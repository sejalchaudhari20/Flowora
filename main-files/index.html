Support & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the product, billing, and how Flowora handles your data securely.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

You get full access to all Pro features for 14 days without entering credit card details. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time directly from your dashboard settings.

Is my data secure with Flowora?

Absolutely. We are SOC-2 Type II compliant and enforce end-to-end 256-bit AES encryption. Your data is yours alone and is never used to train public LLM models.

Can I integrate with my existing tools?

Yes, Flowora connects seamlessly with over 100+ popular applications including Slack, Notion, GitHub, and major databases through our pre-built integrations or standard REST API.

What kind of support do you offer?

All plans include standard email support. Pro Business and Enterprise plans come with priority 24/7 support, dedicated Slack channels, and a dedicated Account Manager.
